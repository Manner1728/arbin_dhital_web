मनको मौनताबाट जन्मिएका शब्दहरू

यो वेबसाइट कुनै साधारण परिचयपत्र होइन। यो कविता, आध्यात्मिक अनुभूति, साहित्यिक खोज र जीवनप्रतिको गहिरो प्रश्नहरूको जीवित अभिलेख हो।

यहाँ प्रकाशित प्रत्येक रचनाले बाहिरी संसारभन्दा परको यात्रातर्फ संकेत गर्छ—जहाँ शब्द विचार बन्छ, विचार ध्यान बन्छ र ध्यान आत्मबोधतर्फ खुल्छ।

भोगको सुखमा दौडिदै गर्दा

आफुलाइ जान्दैमा तिमी गुम्दैनौ  नडराउ
शुन्यमा शुन्यसरी बिलाउदैनौ ।
बरु भरिन्छौ यसअघी खाली थियौ
सुक्ष्मबाट बिराटतामा तिमी अघाउदैनौ ।

What will you gain here?
And what can you lose?
What was never yours is beside you today;
tomorrow, what is with you will belong to another.
Have you ever truly noticed?
The body for which you gather and strive—
it is not who you are.

For an unknown future,
why do you suffer within the familiar present?
You prepare for two brief days,
yet neglect the journey whose destination is unknown.
You have always been filled with divine abundance,
but never wished to know yourself.
Restlessly you seek to consume the world
within this impermanent cycle of birth and death.
Today, even your austerity stands against you,
and your dream is a seed of the inert web.

Do not call a fragment of earth your true self,
yet do not delay in knowing who you are.
You are a burning light, eternally luminous;
do not veil yourself with darkness.
Do not behold the transient world
through the eyes of permanence;
do not cling to what must be left behind.

While running after the pleasure of possession,
you turned away from the supreme nectar.
Do not fear: knowing yourself will not make you disappear.
You will not dissolve into emptiness like emptiness.
Instead, you will become full, where once you were empty;
moving from the subtle into the vast,
you will never be exhausted.

— Arbin Dhital
